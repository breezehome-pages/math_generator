Grade 7 Integer Addition and Subtraction

Worked examples on a number line

Adding a positive integer moves right. Adding a negative integer moves left. To subtract an integer, add its opposite.

Add a negative integer

2 + (−5) = −3

Start at 2. Adding −5 moves you 5 units left to −3.

Subtract a negative integer

−3 − (−5) = −3 + 5 = 2

Start at −3. The opposite of −5 is 5, so move 5 units right to 2.
